## 📌 Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall in Gikomba Market selling tomatoes, onions, sukuma wiki, potatoes, and avocados. Over the past two weeks, she recorded daily sales manually without formal inventory tracking. As her freelance analyst, this project evaluates her 14-day sales data and stock levels using core Python data structures. The analysis reveals a total revenue of **KES 46,450**, a week-on-week growth of **10.66%**, an **82.50% weekend sales premium**, and key inventory reorder alerts for **Tomatoes** and **Sukuma**.
